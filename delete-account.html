Delete your Fathom Blue account

This page explains how to delete your Fathom Blue account and the data linked to it. Fathom Blue is made by FATHOM BLUE & CO LIMITED, Christchurch, New Zealand.

Option 1 — in the app

  1. Open Fathom Blue and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

You'll be signed out straight away. Your account is permanently deleted after 7 days. If you sign back in during those 7 days, you can cancel the request.

Option 2 — by email (no app needed)

Email hello@fathomblueco.com with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address you use to sign in to Fathom Blue. If you can't send from that address, tell us which address your account uses and we'll confirm it's yours before we delete anything.

We will reply within 20 working days. Once we've confirmed the request, your account is deleted the same way as Option 1, after 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your account and everything linked to it: your profile, training history, breath-hold records, session notes, feel ratings, reminder settings and Knowledge Check results.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Backups: copies in backups are overwritten within 30 days of deletion.
  • Waitlist: kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
  • Emails to us: kept for as long as needed to deal with your request.
