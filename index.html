What's Inside

Everything in One App

Six powerful tools, one elegant platform — designed to take you deeper.

🫁
CO₂ & O₂ Training Tables

Personalised breathwork tables that adapt to your maximum breath-hold time. Five protocols — CO₂, O₂, Wonka, Mixed and Custom — plus Box Breathing and dynamic apnea.

📈
Progress Tracking

Log every session. Track your personal bests. Watch your breath-hold times grow with session history, charts and your training streak — all in one place.

🐟
4-Week Training Blocks

Structured block-based training across beginner, intermediate and advanced levels. Each block follows a proven 4-week arc — Accumulation, Progression, Intensification, Deload.

🛡️
Safety First — Always

Buddy system reminders, shallow water blackout education, and a built-in Safety Certification quiz. Safe training protocols are embedded in every single session.

🌍
Three Training Pillars

Every session is one of three pillars — Breathwork, Workout or Mobility. Your daily training combines all three for complete ocean fitness, not just breath-hold numbers.

⌚
Wearable Support

Apple Watch, Android Watch, Garmin and Oximeter integration coming soon. Track your training from your wrist, in or out of the water.
