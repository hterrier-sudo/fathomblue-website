Privacy Policy

Version MVP-1 · Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Fathom Blue collects, why, where it is kept and what you can do about it. It covers the Fathom Blue app and the website fathomblueco.com. It is written for the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

1. Who we are

Fathom Blue is run by Fathom Blue & Co Limited (company number 9451242), Christchurch, New Zealand ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, contact our privacy officer at hello@fathomblueco.com.

2. What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your email address and a password. Passwords are stored by our database provider in hashed form; we never see them.
  • Your name, if you give one.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture link, as Google shares them for basic sign-in. We do not get access to your Google contacts, files, calendar or anything else.

When you set up and use the app

  • Your training profile: experience level, sport goal, workout goal, equipment, training frequency, breathe-up time and the maximum breath-hold you enter.
  • Your training records: the sessions you start and finish, how long they took, rounds and holds completed, breath-hold times and personal records.
  • How a session felt: an optional rating and an optional short note you type.
  • Safety Knowledge Check results: score, pass or fail, reference ID and date.
  • Your reminder schedule: the days and times you choose and whether reminders are on.
  • Safety and account records: when you accepted the safety disclaimer during onboarding and which version, and the date of any account deletion request.

Stored only on your phone

  • Your sign-in session, so you stay signed in.
  • A note that you have finished onboarding, and the date you last accepted the daily safety agreement.
  • Training reminders are scheduled by your phone. They are not sent from our servers.

Collected automatically

  • When the app starts, it checks Expo's update service for app updates. That check sends your platform, app version, update channel and a random install ID, and no account details.
  • Our service providers keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, time and the request made, to run and secure their services.

From the website

  • If you join the waitlist: your email address and where you signed up from. Make.com passes your email to our mail provider, Hostinger, to send you a welcome message.
  • The website uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Vercel, which hosts it, counts page visits: it briefly uses your IP address to make a visitor code that changes every day, so it cannot identify you.

What we do not collect. No location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or health-app data. No advertising IDs. The app has no ads, no ad tracking and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools. During the free test version we take no payments and hold no payment details.

3. Information about your health and fitness

Breath-hold times, session ratings and notes can say something about your health and fitness. We use them only to run your training and show your progress. We never use them to diagnose or assess you, and Fathom Blue does not screen your health. Please do not put medical details in session notes.

4. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To provide your training: choose your next session, set hold lengths from your profile, show your progress and records, and send the reminders you ask for.
  • To keep a record that you accepted the safety disclaimer.
  • To find and fix problems, and to understand whether the test version works, using the training records above. We look at this ourselves; no analytics company receives it.
  • To answer you when you contact us, and to tell you about changes to the app or this policy.
  • To send launch news if you joined the waitlist. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run Fathom Blue. They handle your information only to provide their service to us:

Provider What it does Where
Supabase Database (app data and the website waitlist), sign-in and account deletion Singapore (Southeast Asia region)
Google Sign in with Google, if you choose it; Google Play distributes the app United States and elsewhere
Expo App update service United States
Vercel Hosts the website and counts visits United States and elsewhere
Make.com Passes waitlist sign-ups to our mail provider United States
Hostinger Sends our emails, including the waitlist welcome European Union (France or Germany)

Apple and Google, as app stores, handle your download under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or to a buyer of the business, who would have to keep this policy's promises.

6. Storage outside New Zealand

Your account and training records are stored by Supabase in Singapore. Some providers above are based in the United States, and our emails are stored in the European Union. We choose providers that protect information with safeguards comparable to New Zealand law, and they act on our behalf.

7. Security

  • Information travels encrypted (TLS) and is encrypted at rest by our database provider.
  • Database rules let each account read and change only its own records.
  • Your sign-in session is kept in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Access to the database is limited to the people at Fathom Blue who need it to run the service, and changes go through a review process.

No system is perfectly secure. If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell you and the Privacy Commissioner as the law requires.

8. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open we keep your account and training records.
  • Deleting your account: request it in the app under Profile. After a 7-day grace period, during which you can cancel, your account and every training record linked to it are erased automatically.
  • Backups: copies in backups are overwritten within 30 days of deletion.
  • Waitlist: kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
  • Emails to us: kept for as long as needed to deal with your request.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and to correct it. You can change most of your profile in the app and delete your account there. To make a request, email hello@fathomblueco.com. We will reply within 20 working days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

10. Age

Fathom Blue is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Notifications

Training reminders are optional. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings. We do not send push notifications from our servers.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here with a new date and tell you in the app before an important change takes effect.

13. Contact

Fathom Blue & Co Limited, Christchurch, New Zealand. Email: hello@fathomblueco.com.
