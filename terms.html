3. Safety and your own risk

Breath-hold training can cause blackout, loss of motor control and drowning. An app cannot remove those risks. You agree that:

  • You use the app and train at your own risk.
  • You must never do breath-hold training in or near water alone. You need a trained buddy who can respond to a blackout.
  • You will never hyperventilate (breathe fast and deep) before a hold. It suppresses the urge to breathe without adding oxygen, which makes blackout without warning much more likely.
  • You will do dry training only in a safe place, never while driving, swimming, bathing or anywhere that losing consciousness could hurt you.
  • You will end every hold at the first contraction or urge to breathe, stop if you feel dizzy, unwell or in pain, and never push through warning signs.
  • The app's safety reminders and default settings are aids only. You are responsible for judging whether you are fit to train on any given day.
